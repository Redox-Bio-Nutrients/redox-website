The Redox Bio-Nutrients logo, from redoxgrows.com, and the app icon.

Colours are written on each shape (no `<style>` block), so the files keep their colours wherever they're uploaded. The swoosh is `brand-lime` `#7cc242` (Curtis, 2026-10-28); the BIO-NUTRIENTS line keeps its own `#98c93c`.

- `redox-logo-on-light.svg`: wordmark in brown `#4a4138` (`color-logo-word`), lime swoosh. Use on `color-bg`, `color-panel` and other light grounds.
- `redox-logo-on-dark.svg`: white wordmark, lime swoosh. Use on any coloured ground: `color-slab`, `color-deep`, dark mode.
- `redox-logo-on-light-black.svg`: black wordmark `#1d2320` (`color-ink`), lime swoosh. The slide and print footer version, on cream and white bands (Curtis, 2026-10-30).
- `redox-mark-tile.svg`: the "x" and lime swoosh in white on a forest-green (`#163620`) tile, a shade off `brand-forest`. The favicon and app icon.
- `certified-biostimulant.svg`: certification seal (green `#63a70a`, orange `#ff9012`) for product and marketing material, not the interface.

Never redraw or stretch a mark. Give it clear space of at least the height of the "x". To centre the wordmark visually, centre the "d" (x 80–118 of the 238-wide drawing), not the whole lockup: the leaf overhangs to the right.
