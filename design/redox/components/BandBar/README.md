# BandBar

A lab reading from very low to very high as five steps, filled to the reading.

Static rendition hand-written from apps/web/src/components/Telemetry.tsx (`BandBar`).

- Five 8px steps with 2px gaps; unfilled steps are hatched. Soil test readings fill in ink, short readings (very low, low) in `color-alert`, saturated paste in the accent.
- The band thresholds are lab science and live in code; never invent them.
